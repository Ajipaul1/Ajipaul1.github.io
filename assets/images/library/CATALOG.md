## confident-leader-office-team-background.webp
*Original filename: iStock-2169869893.webp*

**What's in it:** A woman in a cream/beige blazer stands at the edge of a wooden conference table in a modern open-plan office, holding a clipboard and pen, looking off to the side with a calm, confident expression -- not smiling at camera, more candid/observational. Behind her, out of focus, three colleagues (a woman in an olive blouse on a MacBook, two men in business shirts) sit working at the same table. Exposed-ceiling office with warm pendant lighting, glass-walled meeting rooms, leather chairs.

**Emotion / read:** Quiet confidence, leadership, poise. Reads as "the one stepping forward while the team executes behind her" -- executive presence rather than a posed group photo.

**Colors:** Warm neutral palette -- cream/beige blazer, warm wood tones, amber ambient lighting, a single olive-green accent in the background, soft golden bokeh from pendant lights. Skews warm/amber overall.

**Best used for:** Leadership/founder-style sections, "about us," team/culture pages, anywhere the story is "one person leading, a team behind them." High resolution, portrait-oriented -- works well as a large hero-side or split-section image.
