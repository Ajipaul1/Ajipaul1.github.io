What a London Software Quote Is Actually Made Of

Three things worth knowing before you compare numbers.

💷

Most of It Is Labour

A London build quote carries City-rate consultants, a partner margin and an implementation team billing by the day. None of that makes the software better — it makes the invoice bigger.

🧩

The Product Is Rarely the Problem

Published UK list prices for subscription ERP sit near £74–£112 per user per month, and estimates for a 10–20 user cloud rollout commonly reach £1,000–£3,000 a month all-in. The licence is seldom what hurts; the per-seat growth and the change requests are.

🤝

What You Are Really Buying

Whether the people building it understand your workflow, and whether they are still accountable in month nine. That is a question about engineering and communication rather than postcode — and it is the argument we are willing to lose.
