Built for How Manchester Actually Manufactures

Three realities of a Greater Manchester plant that generic ERP ignores.

Subcontract In and Out

Plating, powder coating, heat treatment, laser cutting — so much of the local supply chain runs on work leaving the building and coming back. The system tracks the job while it is out, holds the cost, and does not lose the traceability.

More Than One Site

A unit in Trafford Park and a store in Oldham is a normal shape here. Stock has to be true per site, transfers have to be recorded, and one person has to be able to see the whole picture without walking between buildings.

📶

Industrial-Estate Connectivity

Some units still have unreliable broadband. Scanning and job booking screens are built offline-tolerant and sync when the link returns, so the line does not stop because the internet did.

What Gets Built First

Six modules, in the order that pays for itself fastest.

The audit sets the order for your business. This is the sequence most Greater Manchester manufacturers end up with.

📦

Stock Across Sites

Real quantities per location and bin, reorder points that reflect actual lead times, lot and serial traceability, and barcode scanning that works on a warehouse phone.

📋

Works Orders & Routing

Bills of materials, operations and routing, labour and machine time booked against the job, scrap recorded where it happens rather than guessed at month end.

🛒

Purchasing & Subcontract

Purchase orders with real supplier lead times, subcontract operations tracked while off site, landed cost including duty, and three-way match against goods-in.

💰

Job Costing

True cost of every job when it closes — material, labour, machine, subcontract and overhead — so you can see which customers and which products actually make money.

🧾

Finance Sync & VAT

Two-way sync with Xero or Sage, MTD-ready digital records from order through invoice, and no one re-typing figures between systems.

📊

The Board Report

On-time delivery, margin by job, stock value, WIP and overdue works orders — the numbers a director asks for, produced by the system rather than by someone’s Sunday evening.
