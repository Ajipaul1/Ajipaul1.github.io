What Is Actually Different North of the Border

Three honest answers, including the parts we do not advise on.

🧾

Tax and Accounts: UK-Wide

VAT and Making Tax Digital run through HMRC UK-wide, Companies House is UK-wide, and the accounting stack is the same Xero, Sage and QuickBooks. Nothing special is needed here, and anyone telling you otherwise is selling something.

⚖️

Law: Genuinely Different

Scotland has its own legal system, so contracts, partnerships and property follow Scots law. We build to what your solicitor specifies and we do not pretend to advise on it.

📄

Public Tenders: Their Own Portal

Public-sector work in Scotland runs through Public Contracts Scotland rather than the UK-wide portals, which matters if your pipeline includes council, NHS Scotland or Scottish Government work — the system should produce the documentation those tenders ask for.
