09Before you hire anyone

Six questions for any consultant or company in Kolkata — including us.

This city has good consultants and good agencies. These questions separate the ones who do the work from the ones who sell it, whichever kind you are talking to.

  1. 01

    Who, by name, does the work?

    A consultant answers this in one word. An agency should be able to as well; if the answer is a department, you are buying a process.

  2. 02

    What ships in the first thirty days?

    For most Kolkata businesses the honest answer is the Business Profile and the technical layer. If it is “keyword research”, ask what happens in month two.

  3. 03

    Can I open the staging site now?

    Work in progress is visible or it is not happening yet.

  4. 04

    Why is your price what it is?

    Not “how much” — “why”. The answer should mention your pages, your site’s condition and who ships changes, not a tier name.

  5. 05

    What do I keep if I stop in month three?

    Accounts, code, content, reports. If any of it stays with the supplier, the quoted price is not the price.

  6. 06

    Which results were yours alone?

    Case-study percentages are easy. Ask how much was the client’s own ads, season or team.
