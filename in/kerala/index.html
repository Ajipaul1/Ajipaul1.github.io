09 · Before you hire anyone

Seven questions to ask any SEO expert in Kerala — including us.

Take this list into every conversation. The answers separate people who do the work from people who sell it, and we would rather you hire the right freelancer than the wrong team.

  1. 1

    Who actually makes the code changes?

    If the answer is “your developer”, budget for the queue. If there is no developer, technical recommendations become a PDF.

  2. 2

    What happens in the first thirty days?

    A specific answer — these pages, this fix, this profile — is a plan. “Keyword research and strategy” is a delay.

  3. 3

    Can I see the staging site?

    Anyone doing real work has something to show before it goes live. Anyone who cannot is asking you to trust the invoice.

  4. 4

    What do I keep if I stop after three months?

    Accounts, content, code and reports should be yours from the first day. If they are not, the price you were quoted is not the price.

  5. 5

    Show me a monthly report you sent a client.

    Redacted is fine. What matters is whether it reads like a plan being worked through or a screenshot of rankings.

  6. 6

    Which of these results did you do alone?

    Testimonials and logos are easy to collect. Ask which parts of the work were theirs and which were the client’s own team.

  7. 7

    Will you tell me if I should not hire you?

    The right answer for some Kerala businesses is a good freelancer, or nothing yet. Anyone who never says so is selling, not advising.
