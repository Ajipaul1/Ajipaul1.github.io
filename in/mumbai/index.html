Category architecture

Which collections exist, what they are called, how deep the tree goes. Most Mumbai storefronts rank for nothing because everything sits two clicks too deep.

09Before you hire anyone

Six questions for any SEO agency in Mumbai — including us.

This city has no shortage of suppliers, so the useful question is not who is cheapest but who will show you the work. Take these into every meeting.

01

Who, by name, will do the work?

Not the account manager. If the answer is a team you will never meet, you are buying a process, not people.

02

Can I open the staging site today?

Anyone doing real work has changes to show before they go live. Anyone who cannot is asking you to trust the invoice.

03

What ships in the first thirty days?

A specific list — these pages, this fix, this profile — is a plan. “Research and strategy” is a delay.

04

What do I keep if I stop in month three?

Accounts, content, code, reports: yours from day one, or the quoted price is not the price.

05

Show me one monthly report you sent a client.

Redacted is fine. Does it read like a plan being worked through, or a screenshot of rankings?

06

Which of these case-study numbers were yours alone?

Logo walls are easy. Ask which part of the growth was the agency and which was the client’s own team, ads or season.
