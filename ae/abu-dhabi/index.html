03Who Abu Dhabi is

Six kinds of business, and what each one needs found first.

Abu Dhabi is not Dubai with fewer people. It is a government-and-energy economy with a hospitality coast, a healthcare and education boom in the new districts, and an industrial belt at Mussafah and KIZAD. The first project differs for each.

Government suppliers

Businesses selling to Abu Dhabi entities live by tender portals, the ICV (In-Country Value) programme and a website that has to look like a company that can deliver. English and Arabic, fast, with the certificates findable.

Healthcare & education

Clinics, hospitals, schools and nurseries win on map results, reviews and a Business Profile that is true this month, in both languages.

Real estate

Developers, brokers and property managers competing on brand for terms the big portals own; the winnable shelf is neighbourhood by neighbourhood.

Hospitality & leisure

Yas and Saadiyat hotels, restaurants and experiences competing for booking terms against the OTAs; direct-booking pages that load fast are the whole game.

Industrial & trading

Mussafah, ICAD and KIZAD: contractors, fabricators, traders and logistics firms whose first project is usually the stock and order system, then the site that reads from it.

Culture & tourism

Museums, galleries, tours and the businesses around them, found by international visitors searching in several languages before they land.

04The guarantee problem

One page ranking here promises 100% guaranteed rankings. Here is what can actually be promised.

Google states plainly that no one can guarantee a first-page ranking, and every honest practitioner in Abu Dhabi knows it. A guarantee of positions is either a guarantee of easy terms nobody searches, or a promise that will be renegotiated in month eleven. We think the buyer deserves a different kind of guarantee, and it is the one we give.

Cannot be promised

  • A position for a term, by a date.
  • “60% of keywords ranking in twelve months” — unless the keywords are chosen after the fact.
  • Traffic figures, before anyone has seen the site.

Can be promised, and is

  • What ships in the first thirty days, as a list.
  • A staging URL open every week, a written report every month.
  • Fixes made by the developer on the team, the week they are found.
  • Everything yours — code, accounts, content, reports — from day one.
  • A plain answer when we are not the right buy.

07Before you hire anyone

Six questions for any SEO supplier in Abu Dhabi — including one four hours away.

Take these into every meeting on the Corniche. They apply to us word for word.

  1. 01

    Who, by name, does the work?

    Thirty-year firms with 3,500 clients rank here. Ask which three people are yours and whether you will ever speak to them.

  2. 02

    What exactly is guaranteed?

    Positions cannot be guaranteed by anyone; Google says so. What can be guaranteed is what ships each month. Ask for that list instead.

  3. 03

    Can I open the staging site today?

    Work in progress is visible or it is not happening yet.

  4. 04

    Do you understand ICV and tender portals?

    If you sell to Abu Dhabi entities, your website is read by procurement. Ask how the supplier will make certificates, capability statements and Arabic content findable.

  5. 05

    What do I keep if I stop in month three?

    Accounts, code, content, reports and the plan. If any stays with the supplier, the AED figure is not the price.

  6. 06

    Will you say when a local agency is the better answer?

    Sometimes it is: a government launch that needs people in the room, a brand that needs an Abu Dhabi address on the proposal. Anyone who never says so is selling.
