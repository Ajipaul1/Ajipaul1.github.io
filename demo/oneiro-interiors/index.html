The name

Oneiro is the Greek word for dream. Everyone carries a picture of the home they want. Our work is to take that picture seriously, draw it honestly, and build it so well that walking in feels like remembering.

  • Design

    Layouts, 3D views and materials, drawn around how you live.

  • Build

    Made, installed and finished by one accountable team.

  • Care

    A written warranty, and a phone that gets answered.

How it happens

From first visit to front-door keys.

  1. 01

    Listen

    We visit, measure and ask how you live: who cooks, who hosts, where the shoes go.

  2. 02

    Draw

    Plans, 3D views and a material board, revised until the home on screen is the one in your head.

  3. 03

    Price

    One clear quote, line by line. You approve it before any work begins.

  4. 04

    Build

    Our own team makes and installs everything, with a weekly update and one person to call.

  5. 05

    Hand over

    We clean, check every hinge with you, and hand you the keys and the warranty.
