What the work covers

What does a technical SEO company in Kochi actually fix?

The parts of a website that decide whether Google can find, load and understand it, and the local signals that decide whether you appear in the map results when someone in Kochi searches. Six areas, each changed in the code rather than written up and handed back to you.

⚡

Core Web Vitals on mobile data

Google’s thresholds for a good page are a Largest Contentful Paint within 2.5 seconds, an Interaction to Next Paint of 200 milliseconds or less and a Cumulative Layout Shift of 0.1 or less, at the 75th percentile of visits (source: web.dev, Web Vitals). We measure yours on a mid-range Android and fix images, fonts, scripts and hosting until they pass.

🧭

Crawling and indexing

A sitemap that lists the pages you want found, robots rules that do not block them, canonical tags that pick one version of each URL, and redirects that reach the live page in one hop. Then Search Console tells us which pages Google actually kept.

🧩

Structured data

Schema for your business, services, products, FAQs and breadcrumbs, written to match what the page and your Business Profile say, so Google and AI answers describe you correctly.

📍

Local SEO and Google Business Profile

The right primary category, one address written the same way everywhere, current hours, real photographs, and a page for each part of Kochi you genuinely serve.

🔁

Migrations and redesigns

When a site is rebuilt, every old URL is mapped to its new one before launch, so the rankings the old site earned carry over instead of disappearing into 404 pages.

💬

AEO and GEO

Pages that answer the question in their first sentence and back it with a fact in the second, so Google’s AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity have something specific to cite.

Before anything is written

What should be checked first on a Kochi website?

Indexing, duplicate URLs and mobile speed, in that order. Most ranking problems on small-business websites come from the same short list, and every item on it can be checked in an afternoon. Here it is, in the order we work through it.

  1. Is the site indexed? The pages report in Search Console shows how many of your URLs Google kept and why it dropped the rest. That gap is the first job.
  2. One version of every URL. http and https, www and the bare domain, with and without a trailing slash: all but one should redirect, in a single hop.
  3. Speed on a phone, not a laptop. Loading, responsiveness and layout shift measured on mobile, the way your customers actually meet the site.
  4. Titles that say what and where. “Dental clinic in Edappally” earns the click that “Welcome to our clinic” never will.
  5. Internal links. A page nothing links to is a page Google visits rarely. Every service page should sit within two clicks of the homepage.
  6. Schema that validates. Business, services and FAQs described in structured data that passes Google’s Rich Results Test without errors.
  7. Profile and website agree. Name, address, phone and hours identical on both. A mismatch works against you in the map results.
  8. Old URLs that now fail. Pages that return 404 can keep collecting impressions for months; each should point at the closest live page.

You can check every item on this list yourself. If you would rather we did, the first look is free on a 15-minute call, on one page you choose.

Common Questions About SEO in Kochi

What does a technical SEO company in Kochi actually do?

It fixes the parts of a website that decide whether Google can find, load and understand it: speed on mobile data, crawl paths, indexing, redirects, structured data, and the Google Business Profile that feeds the map results. The difference between a report and a result is who makes the change. At TechAuditPros the developer who finds a problem is the one who fixes it in the code.

How long does a technical SEO review of a Kochi website take?

The first look is free and happens on a 15-minute call, on one page you choose. A written plan for a typical small-business site follows within two to three working days of the full crawl, with the month-one fixes dated, the month-three target stated and the handover terms spelt out.

Where are you based, and can we meet in Kochi?

TechAuditPros is registered at Kothamangalam in Ernakulam district, the same district as Kochi, so a meeting in Kochi city is a drive rather than a flight. Most of the work runs on calls, a staging link you can open every week and a written monthly report; we meet in person when a first conversation or a workshop genuinely needs it.

Can you guarantee page-one rankings in Kochi?

No, and nobody honestly can: Google decides rankings, and a guaranteed position is a promise about something the seller does not control. What we commit to in writing is the work (which fixes ship, on which pages, by when) and a monthly report that shows what moved and what did not.

Is technical SEO different from on-page SEO?

Yes. Technical SEO is the site itself: speed, crawling, indexing, redirects, structured data and how it renders on a phone. On-page SEO is what each page says: titles, headings, copy and internal links. A Kochi business usually needs the technical layer fixed first, because well-written pages on a site Google struggles to load or index still do not rank.

Do you handle Google Business Profile and the map results in Kochi?

Yes, and for Kochi businesses that serve walk-in or phone customers it is usually the first thirty days of work: the right primary category, an address written the same way everywhere it appears, current hours, real photographs and a steady habit of asking for reviews. The website then has to confirm what the profile says.

Will you fix the code, or only send a report?

We fix it. Titles, redirects, templates, image sizes, schema, sitemaps and page speed are changed in the code and shown on a staging link before they go live. If your site runs on a platform we cannot edit, the written plan says exactly what your own developer has to change, page by page.

What are AEO and GEO, and does a Kochi business need them?

They are the work of making a page quotable: AEO for answer boxes and AI Overviews, GEO for chat assistants such as ChatGPT and Perplexity that write an answer and cite a few sources. For a Kochi clinic, resort or supplier that mostly means a clear answer in the first sentence, facts that match your Business Profile, and schema that says who you are.

Do you work in Malayalam?

Conversations can be in Malayalam or English, whichever suits you; written plans and reports are in English. Where your customers search in Malayalam, we build Malayalam pages with the correct language tags instead of machine-translating the English ones.

How much does technical SEO cost for a Kochi business?

It is scoped on a call and put in writing before any work starts, because the price depends on how much is broken rather than on a package name. The written plan tells you what the first thirty days will fix and what you keep if you stop after three months; compare that with any other quote, not only the monthly figure.
